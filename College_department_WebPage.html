About the Department

The Department of Computer Science & Engineering supports students in developing strong computing fundamentals, practical skills, and an enthusiasm for lifelong learning.

Our Vision

To be a center of excellence in computer science education and innovation, preparing graduates to make a positive contribution to society.

Our Mission

  1. Provide a strong foundation in computing principles and emerging technologies.
  2. Develop problem-solving, communication, and collaborative skills through practical learning.
  3. Encourage ethical, inclusive, and socially responsible use of technology.
  4. Promote lifelong learning, research, and engagement with industry and community.
